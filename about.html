About us

Sport, but make it social.

Our mission is to enable recreational as well as ambitious padel and pickleball players to improve their level of play in a professional, yet relaxed and enjoyable environment.

Who we are

Rackets Academy is a modern sports centre dedicated to racket sports and community experiences. We bring together high-quality facilities, experienced coaches, and a friendly atmosphere — from beginners to advanced players.

Beyond the court

Rackets Academy is also a space to relax, connect and share moments, with a bar, wellness area and regular events.
