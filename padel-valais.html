Padel in Valais: good to know

Where can I play padel in Valais?

Rackets Academy has two padel clubs in Valais: in Sion (3 indoor courts) and in Salgesch near Sierre (4 indoor and 2 outdoor courts). Both are open every day from 7:00 to 23:30 and bookable on Playtomic.

How much does padel cost in Valais?

At Rackets Academy a 90-minute indoor game costs 17 CHF per person on weekdays before 5 pm and 20 CHF per person from 5 pm and at weekends, the same in Sion and Salgesch.

Can I play padel indoors in winter?

Yes. Our courts in Sion and four of the courts in Salgesch are indoors, so you can play all year, whatever the weather.

I have never played. Where do I start?

Join the beginner session in Sion every Saturday from 10 to 11 (25 CHF), or book a court with friends and rent rackets on site. Our coaches also give private and group lessons.

Is there padel near Sierre and Crans-Montana?

Yes: our Salgesch club is 5 minutes from Sierre and about 25 minutes from Crans-Montana, with parking in front of the hall.

Do you have tournaments?

Every week: Racketero, plus the monthly Rackemix, Swiss Tennis padel tournaments and special events such as the 24-hour tournament.
