What is pickleball?

Pickleball is a racket sport invented in 1965 in the USA and now one of the fastest-growing sports in the world. It mixes tennis, badminton and table tennis: you play with a solid paddle and a light perforated plastic ball on a small court — usually in doubles. Rallies are short and social, and beginners have fun from the very first game.

📐

Small court

The size of a badminton court (13.4 × 6.1 m), net about 86 cm high.

🏓

Paddle & ball

Solid paddle, light plastic ball with holes — easy on the arm.

⬇️

Underhand serve

Serve diagonally and below the waist; the ball must bounce once on each side first.

🚫

The kitchen

No volleys in the 2.13 m zone next to the net — that makes the game tactical.

🔢

Scoring

Games to 11 points, win by 2. Only the serving team scores.

❤️

Good for you

Low impact on joints, lots of movement and laughter — for all ages.

Pickleball questions

What is pickleball?

A racket sport from the USA that mixes tennis, badminton and table tennis. It is played with a solid paddle and a light perforated plastic ball on a badminton-sized court, usually in doubles. Games go to 11 points.

When is the pickleball Mix & Match?

Every Sunday from 10:00 to 12:00 in Salgesch. Sign up on Playtomic, 10 CHF per person, paddle rental 2 CHF. All levels welcome.

Where can I play pickleball in Valais?

At Rackets Academy in Salgesch: 3 indoor courts, open daily 7:00–23:30, 5 minutes from Sierre, 18 from Sion and 20 from Visp. Book on Playtomic.

How much does pickleball cost?

From 5 CHF per person per hour. A rental paddle costs 2 CHF.

Do I need my own paddle?

No. You can rent a paddle for 2 CHF on site.

Is there pickleball in Sion?

At our Sion site we only have padel. Pickleball is in Salgesch, 18 minutes from Sion.

Is pickleball hard to learn?

No — it is the easiest racket sport to start. Underhand serve, small court, light paddle: most people play real rallies within minutes.

Pickleball or padel?

Both are played in doubles. Pickleball uses a solid paddle and a light plastic ball on a smaller court without walls; padel uses the glass walls. You can try both at Rackets Academy.
