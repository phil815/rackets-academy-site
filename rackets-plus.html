Questions about the membership

What does RACKETS+ cost?

Nothing. The membership is free. All you need is WhatsApp.

Do I need an app?

No. Everything runs in WhatsApp with María, our bot. Your member card sits in Apple Wallet or Google Wallet.

How do I collect points?

Automatically with every booking you make with us in Playtomic: court, class, tournament or spa. On top there are bonus points for your first class, your first tournament, your birthday and for friends you bring.

Do my points expire?

No. Your points stay as long as you are a member.

How is my status calculated?

By the number of days you played in a calendar month: from 4 days Challenger, from 7 Master, from 10 Legend. The status then applies for the whole following month.

How do I leave?

Send STOP to María and you get no more messages. With START you are back, and your points are still there.
