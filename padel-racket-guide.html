Three questions to the right racket

1

Which shape?

Round gives control and forgives off-centre hits. Diamond gives power but needs clean technique. Teardrop sits in between.

2

How hard?

A soft racket is comfortable, easy on the arm and helps the ball out. A hard racket gives more pressure and precision once you hit cleanly.

3

How heavy?

Lighter and balanced towards the handle is easy to move at the net. Heavier and head-heavy puts more weight behind smashes.

Racket questions

Which padel racket is best for beginners?

A round or teardrop racket that is soft to medium and not too heavy. It forgives off-centre hits and is easy on the arm. In our shop these are for example Wilson Blade LS Green, Babolat Veron 2026 Air.

Round, teardrop or diamond: what is the difference?

Round means control and a large sweet spot. Diamond means power, with the weight in the head. Teardrop is the middle: some of both.

How much does a good padel racket cost?

The rackets in our shop currently cost between 180 and 350 CHF. For a beginner a racket around 180–200 CHF is enough.

Can I test a racket before I buy it?

Yes. You rent any racket for 10 CHF and play a real match with it in Salgesch or Sion. If you buy it, the 10 CHF are credited.

I come from tennis. Which racket should I choose?

Tennis players often bring power from their swing, so many feel comfortable with a teardrop racket of medium hardness. The best way is to test two shapes in a match.
