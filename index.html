This is Rackets Academy

Dive into the exciting world of racket sports: play at our academy and create memorable moments in our community. Join matches, book trainings, participate in tournaments, and meet like-minded players.

Padel

Modern indoor and outdoor courts for all, from beginners to competitive players.

Pickleball

A fast-growing and social sport, easy to learn and fun for all ages.

Tennis

Indoor courts allowing you to play comfortably all year round.

Camps

Training camps, MASTERclasses and sports weekends led by experienced coaches.

Spa | Sauna

Relax and recover in our wellness area with sauna, hammam and jacuzzi.

Events

Monthly events combining sport, food and social moments. Also bookable privately.

Bar

Enjoy an after-game drink with your team, or get a fresh pizza or empanada.

Shop

Need an update to your gear? We stock everything from new balls to rackets.
