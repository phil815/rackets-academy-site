Good to know

📍

Where

Rackets Academy Salgesch, Littenstrasse 30 — 5 min from Sierre, 15 min from Crans-Montana, 20 min from Sion and Visp.

👧

Who

Kids aged 6–14, beginners and players. Groups by age and level, max. 20 kids per week.

🎒

What to bring

Indoor sports shoes, sportswear, a water bottle and a snack. Rackets and balls are provided.

Questions from parents

How much does the kids padel camp cost?

CHF 250 per week (5 days, 3 hours a day). The Easter camp has 4 days and costs CHF 200. Rackets and balls are included.

Is it a day camp?

Yes — a sports day camp without overnight stay: 3 hours a day in Salgesch, 5 min from Sierre, 15 min from Crans-Montana, 20 min from Sion and Visp. Ideal alongside ski school or other holiday activities.

When does the camp take place?

January and February: afternoons 14:00–17:00, so kids can ski in the morning. Easter and summer: mornings 9:00–12:00.

Does my child need to know how to play padel?

No. Kids are grouped by age and level, so beginners and players both get the right coaching.

How many kids are in a camp?

Maximum 20 kids per week, split into small groups by age and level.

How do I book?

Online on this page: choose the week, enter your children’s names and ages and pay by card or TWINT. You get the confirmation by email right away.
