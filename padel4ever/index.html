THE RULES

Four rules. That's the whole format.

RULE 01

THE COURT CAN NEVER GO EMPTY

Two players on court, non-stop, for 24 hours straight. Miss your shift and you're done.

RULE 02

ONE SCOREBOARD. HIGHEST WINS

Every point stacks onto your team's score. No brackets, no sets to protect.

RULE 03

EVERY PARTNERSHIP. ONCE.

8 players, 28 unique duos. Every partnership plays together — once each.

RULE 04

LOSE 3, KEEP GOING. LOSE 4, YOU'RE OUT.

8 players give depth. Drop to 4 and the team can't safely cover 24 hours.
