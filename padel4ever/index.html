How it works

Read this once. Then play it below, and it clicks.

Build a crew of eight

One captain, seven teammates. Mixed levels are fine. Depth matters more than one star.

Two of you are always on court

All six crews play at the same time on three courts. Each round lasts 45 minutes plus a 5-minute change. You face a different crew every round.

The captain picks the duo

Before each round the captain sends two players. Rule one: every possible duo of your eight plays exactly once (that's 28 rounds). Rule two: nobody plays two rounds in a row.

Every game counts

No sets to protect, no knockouts. Every game your duo wins is a point on the live table. Lose 6–4? You still bank 4.

×2

Graveyard shift

Rounds 16 to 19, from 01:30 to 04:50. Every game counts double for everyone. Whoever saves energy for the night wins the tournament.

×2

Joker

Once per tournament the captain can double one round for their crew only. Not in the graveyard shift. Timing is everything.

×2

Final round

Sunday 12:20 to 13:00. Captain's pick, any duo, double points. The table can still flip in the last 40 minutes.

Enter your crew, then pick the duo for every round. Watch energy, save players for the graveyard shift, and use your joker. The other five crews are run by the computer.

Questions

Do I have to stay awake for 24 hours?

No. You play about every fourth round. Most players sleep 3 to 4 hours in shifts. Your captain plans who rests when.

What level do I need?

You should be comfortable in a match. Crews of mixed levels work well, because every duo plays once and the captain decides when.

What if a player gets injured or drops out?

A crew needs at least five fit players to continue. With fewer, the crew stops and keeps the points it has.

How is the score counted?

Each round is 45 minutes of play. Every game your duo wins is one point, two in the graveyard shift, the final round or your joker round. Ties are broken by total games won.

Can I register alone?

Yes. Message us and we put you in a crew.
