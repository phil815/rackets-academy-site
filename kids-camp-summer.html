Good to know

📍

Where

Rackets Academy Salgesch, Littenstrasse 30 — 5 min from Sierre, 20 min from Visp.

👧

Who

Kids aged 6–14, beginners and players. Groups by age and level.

🎒

What to bring

Indoor sports shoes, sportswear, a water bottle and a snack. Rackets and balls are provided.

Questions from parents

How much does the kids padel camp cost?

CHF 250 per week (5 mornings, Monday to Friday, 9:00–12:00). Short weeks with 4 mornings cost CHF 200. Rackets and balls are included.

Does my child need to know how to play padel?

No. Kids are grouped by age and level, so beginners and players both get the right coaching.

Which age group is the camp for?

For kids and teens aged 6 to 14.

Do the camp weeks match our school holidays?

The weeks follow the Valais school holidays. If your holidays fall on other dates, write us on WhatsApp and we will find a solution.

How do I book a spot?

Tap “Reserve a spot” next to the week you want and send us the WhatsApp message with your child’s name and age. We confirm your spot and send the payment details.
